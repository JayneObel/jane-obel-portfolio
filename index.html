My approach

Simple steps. Useful systems.

I do not begin with a tool. I begin by understanding the people, the work and the decisions the system needs to support.

01

Understand

Map the programme, participant journey, stakeholders and operational gaps.

02

Design

Build the workflow, responsibilities, communication journey and reporting structure.

03

Deliver

Coordinate people, timelines, tools and participant support.

04

Improve

Use feedback and data to find risks, remove friction and strengthen delivery.
